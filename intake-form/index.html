Patient Intake Form

Please complete this 2-page form before your appointment.

Download the Intake Form (PDF)

How to complete it

  1. Download the form and print it, or fill it in on your phone or computer with a PDF app.
  2. Answer every question on both pages, including your medical history and allergies.
  3. Sign and date page 2.
  4. Email the completed form to langleyoms@gmail.com (a photo or scan of each page is fine), or bring it to your appointment.
Please note: 48 hours' notice is required to change an appointment, otherwise a $75 cancellation fee applies.

Privacy: Regular email isn't encrypted. If you'd prefer not to send health information by email, please bring your completed form to your appointment.

Questions? You're welcome to text or call us at 604-371-0578.
